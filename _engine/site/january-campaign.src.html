Why this exists as its own thing

Everything sold as a monthly membership in January expires in March. A gym that knows that sells terms in January. A gym that doesn't calls March a bad month. That is why a January campaign has to be commissioned in November — and why the deadline renews honestly, every year.

What we build together

  • One thing your members can actually say yes to from a phone — small enough to buy without a call.
  • Thirty days of content built to sell it: five pillars on a five-day cycle, every day ending at the same offer.
  • A trained front desk running the sales script when the campaign goes live.
  • A count of everyone who names the offer — the number that makes the guarantee enforceable.

How it runs

  1. Find the strength — your numbers plus short review calls with your own members from my script.
  2. Build the offer — one simple, named, dated challenge built on that strength.
  3. Test it on current members first — one week, before a single post is made. If they will not buy it, we fix it before thirty days of content are built on top of it.
  4. Thirty days of content — five pillars on a five-day cycle. Every day has an angle and every angle ends at the same offer.
  5. Brief the desk — sales scripts, and your existing staff trained to use them, so what the content brings does not leak at the last step.
  6. Read the numbers back — anyone who walks in has to name the offer. For the first time, the posting has a countable end.

The guarantee

If this campaign does not bring you five new subscribers in six weeks — the number where it has paid for itself at your own published price — I build the next one free.

The number to target is fifty. The number I guarantee is five. Guaranteeing the target is how one fee buys two campaigns. Guaranteeing break-even is how you know I mean it.

What we ask of you

  • Post at least twenty-four of the thirty planned posts.
  • The offer live at the desk and in the bio by day one.
  • Your trained staff run the script on enquiries.
  • Log everyone who names the offer.

The last one is the most important sentence on this page. Without a count, the guarantee is unenforceable and the case study is mush.

Branches

25,000 covers one brand. Additional branches: 10,000 each. Not volume pricing. Each branch needs its own localised offer, its own desk brief and its own count. Their trained staff can run it — what the fee buys is the branch being measurable.

This is the seasonal middle tier. Most of the year the ladder is two: the audit and the four-month fill. This one shows up in October, sells for two months, delivers into January, and disappears again.
