What this is

A fixed-term consulting engagement that delivers the outcomes an in-house social-media role was written to produce. Four months. Reporting to the MD. Weekly review of what shipped and what didn't. Monthly written report against numbers agreed at kickoff. Definite end date.

It is a fill, not a hire

This exists because there is a moment where the choice a gym group faces is "hire a full-time social-media manager and hope" or "buy an agency retainer and never get out." Both fail for the same reason: neither one starts by building the thing your content is being asked to sell.

What I do instead: I fill the function for four months, build the offer the campaign sells in month one, run the campaign in months two through four, and hand it over at the end — either as a hiring spec you use to make your next hire well, or as evidence you don't need one at all.

The first month is not content. It is offer formulation.

You have to do sales because you are poor at marketing. You have to do marketing because you are poor at product development — you have no offer.

Every gym marketing brief starts one link too late in that chain. Post more, run ads, get more enquiries is the marketing layer. The gyms this offer is written for already do that layer well enough. What their content cannot sell is that every plan they publish is a duration. No student, no couple, no time of day, no kind of person. So every post has one thing to ask for, and it is the biggest thing.

Month one rebuilds the ask. I design the offer — the challenge, the promise, the price, the qualification, the guarantee — then the campaign that sells it. Months two through four run it and iterate.

How month one actually works — the free scripts do the heavy lifting

Marketing FM already owns two script libraries. Both are included at no extra fee. Member-review call scripts — a structured twenty-minute conversation with five to eight of your own current members. Desk-side script library — sales lines the front desk actually uses when the campaign goes live. Neither script asks you to buy or build anything. They travel with the engagement and survive the handover as documented assets. This is what makes the offer-formulation deliverable possible in four weeks and not four months.

What each side puts in

KARL PROVIDES: strategy · content calendar · ad ops · sales-script coordination · monthly report · month-1 offer build · the two script libraries YOU PROVIDE: MD as counterpart · gym access on shoot days · trainer/member availability with signed consent · ad budget released to Karl-directed spend · desk-staff time for briefing · weekly implementation of agreed changes

Where I work from

On-site two days a week — shoot days plus one desk-and-review day. Remote for planning, scheduling, ad ops and reporting. Travel is factored into the fee.

The guarantee

In the first 8 weeks I deliver the agreed campaign structure, live at the desk and on the feed. If it does not bring 5 people who name the current offer at the desk in that window, I refund my fee for the 8-week period.

Conditional on you holding your side: ad budget funded, consents signed, desk briefed, weekly changes implemented, MD attends the review.

The math

Your average subscription is around 6,000 shillings a month. Five new named-offer members at the annual rate come to about 480,000 shillings over a year. The engagement fee is 264,000. The number pays for itself on the guarantee floor, not on the target.

The exit at month 4

A documented playbook you own — the pillars, the calendar cadence, the shoot workflow, the ad-set structure, the reporting template, the desk-script library, and one worked month of examples. Written so somebody else can run it. Plus one of two exit routes: a hiring spec for the SMM you hire next (I help screen the first three candidates if asked), or evidence the system runs without a full-time hire.

Optional add-on to the handover

A configured openGym instance — the open-source workout tracker, branded and populated for your members, with printed twelve-week programme series generated from it as PDFs. Priced separately from the engagement fee. The AGPL licence terms and the sign-off live in the contract.

Filter

I take one client at a time. Ideally gyms with a live website, more than one location or a parent brand, prices published on the page, and somebody whose job it is to run marketing. The first fit sits below every other filter: you can decide.
