What this looks like in practice

Four teardowns, all real, none invented

Each of these is a piece of delivered work — not for a gym, in three of the four cases, but the craft is the same. Every number below is computed from what the subject published or posted. Nothing here is a projection.

31 July 2026 · @rugsbysensei · handmade rugs

People love the rugs. Then they leave.

Twelve videos, roughly twenty-five thousand views, a like rate most accounts never reach. Almost nobody followed. The bio said "DM to order" and nothing else — no price, no link. A stranger who wanted a rug had no idea whether it cost three thousand shillings or thirty. The recommendation was not to make better videos. It was to put a price and a one-tap link in the bio, and to sell the four rugs already finished before selling "custom" — because custom asks a stranger to pay upfront and wait weeks.

12 videos · ~25,000 views · 9.0% liked · 0.41% followed

23 July 2026 · Zelha Spin & Fitness, Juja · a gym

Which of a gym's creators are worth contracting.

Zelha works with a circle of coaches and clients who post to their own TikToks. Five of them were scored as fitness-influencer candidates — on their gym content only — using engagement, reach efficiency, and how often the gym already reposted them. The report ended in a decision, not a dashboard: one creator to contract first, one small account to trial because its videos travelled about nine times its own following, and one named as not a fit yet, with the reason.

840 videos analysed · 533 gym-relevant · 5 creators scored · 23 reposts mapped

July 2026 · @sidneynyaga · forex mentorship

He spends the most effort on his weakest topic.

The entire catalogue — 1,829 videos over three years, 11.6 million views — read by theme and by month. Not a sample. The question it was built to answer was narrow: what should he make more of, and when? Half of everything posted sat in the theme that performed below his own average. The theme that beat it by almost double was answering questions, and it was a sixth of the output.

1,829 videos · 11.6M views · 6,349 average · 2,145 median

September 2026 · two Nairobi gym groups · published prices

Every plan is a duration.

Two multi-branch Kenyan gym groups, read from their own public price pages and recomputed to a per-month rate — which neither of them publishes, which is why nobody notices what it shows. Not one plan is a kind of person, and not one is a time of day. A day pass, a month, three, six, twelve. Four ways to buy the same thing and no way to buy a different thing. Every post has one offer to point at, and it is the biggest one. Doubling a six-month commitment to a full year saves the member 8.3%. The cash-flow product has almost nothing to argue with.

2 groups · 4 price lists · 6 defects named · year saves 8.3% over 2×6mo
